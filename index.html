What this film is

This is the presentation cut of the Ergon demo film — 14 FILMABLE NOW scenes from a real end-to-end run on the Ergon demo company environment: an invented warehouse company with lettered board lines, crews on the road, dump runs and a morning job flow, all on invented demo data only. The company is a demo; Ergon is company-agnostic.

The 7 NEEDS BUILD scenes (5, 8, 10, 12, 14, 19, 20) are not footage — they are a build-requirements list, and they are not in this film and are not faked.

Re-shot for this cut: scenes 02, 03, 13, 15, 17 and 18. Borrowed and named in the cut manifest: scenes 06 and 13. Nothing in the film is staged: every scene is a real continuous take on a running demo build, and the cut manifest carries each take's sha256 and run evidence.
